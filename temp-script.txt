MUSAFIR AI - APP REVIEW SCREEN RECORDING SCRIPT
================================================
Target length: 3-4 minutes. One continuous take, no cuts.

BEFORE YOU PRESS RECORD
- Use a physical iPhone on the latest iOS (not simulator). Control Centre > Screen Recording. Turn the mic ON if you want to narrate.
- Install the exact build submitted to App Review (TestFlight build).
- Delete the app first so the recording starts from a fresh launch and shows permission prompts.
- Create a throwaway test account for the deletion demo (do NOT delete the demo account you give Apple).
- Have good internet, the volume up, and a route with already-generated stories ready (for example Bengaluru -> Mysore Palace).
- Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notifications pop up.
- Keep the phone in light mode for the whole take.

SCENE 1 - LAUNCH AND ONBOARDING (0:00 - 0:30)
Say: "This is Musafir AI, a voice-first road-trip companion. It plays short audio stories about history, culture and food as you drive, in your own language."
1. Tap the app icon on the home screen (the recording must start BEFORE this).
2. Show the splash, then onboarding.
3. Pick a language (for example Kannada or Hindi) and show that there are 11 Indian languages.
4. Pick a voice / mood / audio preference if onboarding asks.

SCENE 2 - ACCOUNT REGISTRATION AND LOGIN (0:30 - 1:00)
Say: "Users can sign up with email, Google, or Sign in with Apple."
1. Show the login screen.
2. Show the Sign in with Apple and Google buttons.
3. Create the account: tap sign up, enter email and password, submit. (Or tap Sign in with Apple and complete it.)
4. Land on the Home tab.

SCENE 3 - PERMISSIONS (1:00 - 1:15)
Say: "The app asks for location so it can play stories for the place you are passing, and the microphone for voice questions."
1. Tap Allow on the location permission prompt (While Using the App).
2. Tap Allow on the microphone prompt when it appears (or when you first ask a question in Scene 5).

SCENE 4 - PLAN A TRIP AND PLAY STORIES (1:15 - 2:15)
Say: "I choose a start and a destination. The app builds a route and narrates stories along it."
1. On Home, use the source / destination search: type "Bengaluru", pick it, then type "Mysore Palace", pick it.
2. Show the generated trip and the route on the map.
3. Open the trip. Show the list of stories along the route.
4. Tap Preview on a story to play its audio. Let it play 10-15 seconds.
5. Start the trip. Say: "As the GPS moves along the route, the next story plays automatically when I reach that place."
6. Show the audio player, the story title and the current-location marker on the map.
   Note for the reviewer: if you cannot drive during the recording, tap a story's play control (or use your demo / simulated location) to show a story triggering.

SCENE 5 - VOICE Q&A (2:15 - 2:40)
Say: "I can also ask questions by voice. There is no text chatbot."
1. Tap the microphone / ask button.
2. Ask a short question in your language, for example "Tell me about Mysore Palace." (keep it under 30 seconds)
3. Show the spoken answer playing.

SCENE 6 - CONTENT REPORTING (2:40 - 3:05)
Say: "All the stories are AI-generated. Users can report any content that is inappropriate or inaccurate."
1. On a story (or the live Q&A transcript), tap the report / flag option.
2. Show the report screen with reasons (inappropriate, inaccurate, offensive, harmful, hate speech, copyright, other).
3. Pick a reason, add a short note, submit.
4. Show the "Report Submitted" confirmation.

SCENE 7 - PROFILE, SUPPORT AND LEGAL (3:05 - 3:20)
1. Open the Profile tab.
2. Show Support, Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links.
3. Show language / voice preferences, and the light / dark theme.

SCENE 8 - ACCOUNT DELETION (3:20 - 3:45)
Say: "Account deletion is available inside the app."
1. Profile tab > Delete Account.
2. Read out the warning, confirm the deletion with the throwaway account.
3. Show that the app signs out and returns to the login screen.

SCENE 9 - WRAP UP (3:45 - 4:00)
Say: "That is Musafir AI: location-triggered audio stories and voice Q&A in 11 Indian languages. There are no in-app purchases and no paid content." (Edit this line if that is not true.)
Stop the recording.

AFTER RECORDING
- Watch it once to check nothing private (real email, notifications) is visible.
- Upload it (a link on Google Drive / YouTube unlisted, or attached in the reply) in the App Store Connect reply AND paste the same link into App Review Information > Notes.
- In Notes, also include:
  * Purpose and audience (see the earlier reply draft).
  * Demo account email and password.
  * Steps to reach the main features (this script, short form).
  * External services: Gnani Vachana (TTS/STT), Google Gemini (story generation), Google Maps Platform (Routes, Places), Wikipedia, MongoDB Atlas, Firebase Auth.
  * Regional note: works anywhere, optimised for India and 11 Indian languages.
  * "No regulated industry. No protected third-party material. Content is AI-generated from public sources."

THINGS TO VERIFY ON YOUR BUILD BEFORE RECORDING
- The exact labels of the buttons above (I wrote them from the code structure, so adjust to match what you see on screen).
- Whether the Sign in with Apple button shows on iOS (it must, if you offer Google sign-in).
- That the report flow and Delete Account both work on the submitted build.


TECHNOLOGIES AND EXTERNAL SERVICES USED
(Paste this into the Notes field and the reply to App Review. Apple asks for item 4: list of external services.)

MOBILE APP (client)
- Expo / React Native with TypeScript, Expo Router for navigation
- react-native-maps (map and route display)
- expo-location and expo-task-manager (GPS and geofencing to trigger stories)
- expo-audio (story and answer playback and voice recording)
- react-native-reanimated, gesture-handler, expo-image, expo-haptics (UI)
- AsyncStorage and expo-file-system (preferences and offline audio cache)
- Firebase Authentication client SDK, with Sign in with Apple (expo-apple-authentication) and Google Sign-In

BACKEND (our own server)
- Node.js, Express and TypeScript
- zod (request and response validation)
- MongoDB Atlas via Mongoose (database)
- Firebase Admin SDK (verifies user login tokens)
- Backblaze B2 through the S3 API (AWS SDK v3) for storing generated audio, served by signed URLs
- ffmpeg (audio processing and joining mp3 chunks)

THIRD-PARTY AI AND DATA SERVICES
- Gnani Vachana (Timbre v2.5): text-to-speech narration and speech-to-text for voice questions, in 11 Indian languages
- Google Gemini API (@google/genai): generates the story scripts and answers questions
- Google Maps Platform: Routes API (route) and Places API (nearby places)
- Wikipedia API: public facts used as source material for stories
- Firebase Authentication (Google): sign-in and account identity
- Sign in with Apple: sign-in

DATA AND PRIVACY NOTES
- API keys stay on our server; the app never contains them.
- Audio and transcripts of voice questions are sent to Gnani for transcription and to Gemini for answers.
- Location is used only to pick stories along the route.
